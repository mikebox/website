Power Play 2047

Decarbonising Without Slowing Down
Day 2 · Sat 14 Nov 202615:00 – 16:00IIM Bangalore, BengaluruTentative schedule
Register on Unstop ↗Full agenda
The central question

What must India do in the next decade to lower emissions without compromising growth, energy access or strategic autonomy?

The premise

Why this conversation, why now.

India’s 2047 ambition will be energy-intensive: more manufacturing, expanding cities, digital infrastructure and rising aspirations will all increase demand. At the same time, India has set a net-zero goal for 2070.

The challenge is not whether to grow or decarbonise, but how to do both while keeping power reliable, energy affordable and industry globally competitive.

Directions for the conversation

Six directions to explore.

01

Firm, affordable clean power

How renewables, storage, transmission, grid flexibility and nuclear can deliver dependable energy.

02

Decarbonising demand

Pathways for electric mobility, steel, cement, chemicals and other hard-to-abate sectors.

03

Next-generation fuels

Where green hydrogen, bioenergy and emerging technologies can make a commercial difference.

04

Energy security & industrial capability

Resilience in critical minerals, batteries, clean-energy equipment and fuel supply chains.

05

Financing the transition

Bankable projects, healthier utilities and affordable energy for industry and households.

06

The decisive moves to 2035

Reforms, investments and partnerships that avert carbon lock-in and create new industries and jobs.
