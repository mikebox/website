India Stack 2047

From Digital Infrastructure to Digital Empowerment
Slot to be confirmedIIM Bangalore, BengaluruTentative schedule

The central question

How can India turn digital access into real gains in opportunity, productivity and quality of life?

The premise

Why this conversation, why now.

India’s Digital Public Infrastructure — Aadhaar, UPI and other shared rails — made identity, payments and service delivery work at population scale. By 2047, open rails spanning health, education, commerce, credit and governance could bring citizens better services and small businesses wider markets.

But digital reach is not the same as digital empowerment.

Directions for the conversation

Six directions to explore.

01

From access to empowerment

What must change for digital access to become better livelihoods, services and choices?

02

The next public rails

Where can shared rails unlock most value across health, education, commerce and credit?

03

Open by design

How can interoperability, open protocols and consent-based exchange prevent silos?

04

Public rails, private innovation

How can startups, MSMEs and enterprises build businesses and create jobs on open rails?

05

Trust & inclusion

How can privacy, cybersecurity, accessibility and multilingual design make DPI work for everyone?

06

The next 3–5 years

Which decisive moves by government, industry and academia will turn digital scale into measurable outcomes?
