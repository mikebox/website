The Next Frontier

Shaping India’s Deep-Tech Future
Slot to be confirmedIIM Bangalore, BengaluruTentative schedule
Register on Unstop ↗Full agenda
The central question

How do promising prototypes become globally competitive enterprises?

The premise

Why this conversation, why now.

India’s aspirations for 2047 will be shaped not only by how quickly it adopts technology, but by whether it can invent, commercialise and scale breakthroughs of its own. From space and advanced energy systems to AI, mobility and healthcare, Indian ventures are tackling problems where scientific depth matters as much as speed.

Yet deep tech runs on a different clock: long R&D cycles, capital-intensive validation, hard-to-win first customers and uncertain paths to scale.

Directions for the conversation

Six directions to explore.

01

From breakthrough to business

De-risking technology, proving reliability and crossing the gap between prototype and commercial scale.

02

Capital for the long game

Finding patient funding, credible milestones and partners willing to underwrite technical risk.

03

The first-customer challenge

Turning enterprise and government pilots into adoption, procurement and repeatable demand.

04

India’s capability stack

Building the talent, IP, testbeds, manufacturing depth and supply chains needed to scale.

05

The lessons behind the wins

Candid accounts of experiments that failed, pivots that worked and difficult trade-offs.

06

Building for the world

Where India can lead in original technology, and the decisive moves needed in the next 3–5 years.
