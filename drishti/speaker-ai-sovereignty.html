AI Sovereignty

Can India Own Its AI Future?
Day 2 · Sat 14 Nov 202616:20 – 17:20IIM Bangalore, BengaluruTentative schedule
Register on Unstop ↗Full agenda
The central question

Must India own every layer of the AI stack, or build the strategic freedom to innovate, deploy and govern AI through domestic capability, open ecosystems and trusted partners?

The premise

Why this conversation, why now.

Artificial intelligence is becoming strategic infrastructure, shaping productivity, public services, national security and economic power. India brings real strengths: digital public infrastructure, a deep technology talent base, multilingual demand and a growing AI ecosystem backed by the IndiaAI Mission. Yet dependencies in advanced chips, frontier-scale computing, patient capital and proprietary platforms pose difficult choices.

Sovereignty is not isolation. It is the freedom to choose, build, deploy and govern AI on India’s terms.

Directions for the conversation

Six directions to explore.

01

The AI stack

Which parts of chips, compute, cloud, data, models and applications must India control?

02

Build or partner?

Is complete self-reliance realistic, or is resilient interdependence a better goal?

03

India’s edge

Where can Indic-language, domain-specific and population-scale AI create global leadership?

04

Economics of scale

What will unlock affordable compute, research depth, patient capital and reliable energy?

05

Trust & governance

How can India protect data, security and public trust while enabling competition and innovation?

06

The next 3–5 years

Which decisive moves by government, industry and academia cannot wait until 2047?
